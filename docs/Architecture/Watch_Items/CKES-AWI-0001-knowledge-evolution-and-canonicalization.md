## Question

How should candidate knowledge become canonical knowledge, and how should canonical knowledge evolve through governed commit mechanisms?

## CKES Experimental Approach

- Staging and controlled canonical commit pipeline (`poc/packages/pipeline`)
- Candidate → policy → validation → commit separation
- Progressive corpus experiments measuring canonical novelty rate

## Success Criteria

- Measurable decline in new concepts per 1,000 source items as knowledgebase matures
- Auditable change sets with rationale and evidence
